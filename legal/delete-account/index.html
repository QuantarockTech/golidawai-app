Delete your account

Last updated 10 September 2026

You can ask us to delete your GoliDawayi account and the information stored with it at any time. You do not need to give a reason.

How to ask

Send an email from the address your account uses, so we can be sure the request is yours, with the subject “Delete my account”.

ravi@golidawayi.com

If you can no longer access that email address, write to us from any address and include your registered mobile number — we will confirm your identity another way before deleting anything.

You can also ask by calling the pharmacy on +91 9755517194.

What we may keep, and why

Orders you have already sent reach the pharmacy as WhatsApp messages. Those messages sit in the pharmacy’s own WhatsApp history and are not part of your app account, so deleting your account does not remove them. The pharmacy may also need to keep a record of medicines dispensed where Indian law requires it.

If you want your details removed from the pharmacy’s WhatsApp records as well, say so in your email and we will pass the request on.

How long it takes

We aim to delete your account within 7 days of confirming your request, and we will email you when it is done. Deletion is permanent — your address, order history and saved contacts cannot be recovered afterwards, and signing up again starts a new, empty account.

Deleting less than everything

If you only want to remove part of your information, you can do that yourself in the app without deleting your account: your delivery address, family members and emergency contact can each be edited or removed on the profile screen.
