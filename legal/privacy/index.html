How we use it

  • To prepare, confirm and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about an order — by phone or on WhatsApp.
  • To show you your saved address and past orders on any device you sign in to.
  • To find and fix crashes and errors in the app.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Prescriptions and health information

A prescription is health information about you, and we treat it as confidential. You should understand exactly how it travels:

Prescription images are uploaded to imgbb, a third-party image host, and the pharmacy receives a link to them. These links are long and unguessable, and they stop working 30 days after the order is sent. They are not, however, protected by a password — anyone who obtains the link within those 30 days could open the image.

We tell you this plainly so you can decide what you are comfortable sending. If you would rather not upload a prescription, you can call the pharmacy on +91 9755517194 instead.

Who else sees your information

The pharmacy

Your order is sent to the pharmacy over WhatsApp. That message contains the medicines you asked for, your delivery address, your mobile number, and links to any prescription images. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also handled under WhatsApp’s own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Service providers

  • Clerk — creates and secures your account, and handles sign-in, including Google sign-in.
  • Supabase — stores your saved address, order history, family members and emergency contact, so they follow you between devices.
  • imgbb — hosts prescription images, as described above.
  • PostHog (European servers) — records app events such as sign-in, sign-up, password reset and crashes, so we can tell whether the app is working. It does not receive your prescriptions, your address or your order contents.

We share information with these providers only so they can perform these functions for us. We do not otherwise disclose your information, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Prescription image links stop working 30 days after the order is sent.
  • Your account, address and order history are kept while your account exists, so you can see what you ordered before.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we remove this information as described below.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your information — your address, family members and emergency contact can be edited or removed in the app at any time.
  • Location — sharing it is optional. You can type your address instead, and you can withdraw the permission in your device settings.
  • Delete your account and data — see Delete your account, or write to ravi@golidawayi.com.

Security

Information travels between the app and our services over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your saved address and order history can only be read by your own signed-in account. No system is perfect, and the limits of the prescription links are described plainly above rather than left for you to discover.

Children

GoliDawayi is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information. An adult may of course order medicines on behalf of a child in their family.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
